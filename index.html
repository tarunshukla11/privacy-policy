🛡️ 1. Core Commitment & Philosophy

Life & Lessons is built on a simple foundation: Complete User Privacy. We believe daily wisdom and inspirational perspective should be calm, distraction-free, and private.

Our principles are unambiguous:

  • Zero Account Requirements: You never need to register, create a profile, or enter an email address.
  • Zero Tracking & Advertising: We do not track you, profile your behavior, or serve advertisements.
  • Offline-First: All 256 curated quotes, favorites, and settings function locally on your device without transmitting data over the internet.

📊 2. Information We Collect (Or Do Not Collect)

Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, physical locations, IP addresses, or payment details.

Local Device Storage: The following preferences are kept strictly within your device's private sandboxed storage (via Android SharedPreferences and browser localStorage):

  • Saved Favorites: The thoughts you bookmark with the heart icon.
  • Shuffle Memory: Recently viewed quote IDs used to prevent consecutive repeats.
  • Themes & Fonts: Your preferred ambient palette (Gold, Aurora, etc.) and typeface.
  • Wallpaper Preferences: Chosen wallpaper schedule (Daily, Screen-Lock mode) and target screens.

This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server.

📱 3. Android Permissions & Justification

The Android version of the app requests only the minimal permissions required to deliver its native capabilities:

Permission Android API Reason for Use
Set Wallpaper android.permission.SET_WALLPAPER Applies wisdom quote wallpapers to Home Screen, Lock Screen, or both, including automated daily or screen-lock updates.
Boot Completed android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restores your chosen wallpaper schedule and refreshes Home Screen Widgets after your device restarts.
Notifications android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Delivers your optional daily thought reminder at your scheduled morning time. No spam or marketing messages.
Internet android.permission.INTERNET Standard web container requirement for Capacitor. The application library works 100% offline.
Storage (Legacy) android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Only used on older Android versions if you tap "Export Collection" or save a wallpaper to your Downloads.

🖼️ 4. Home Screen Widgets & Screen-Lock Automation

Native Android Widgets: Small (2x2), Medium (4x2), and Large (4x3) widgets render through native Android RemoteViews. They read quotes from the local offline database and do not collect any usage data.

Screen-Lock Wallpaper Mode: If you enable "Every time screen is locked" in Wallpaper Studio, a native background receiver listens locally for Intent.ACTION_SCREEN_OFF to apply a fresh quote when you lock your device. It runs for less than 50ms, requires no wake locks, and works fully offline.

🔒 5. Third-Party Services & Children's Privacy

No Third-Party SDKs: We do not include third-party trackers, ad networks (such as Google AdMob or Unity Ads), or behavioral analytics.

Children's Privacy (COPPA): Life & Lessons is suitable for all ages and contains no harmful or sensitive material. We do not collect information from children under 13.

🗑️ 6. User Control & Data Deletion

You have complete control over all stored data:

  • You can clear all saved favorites at any time in Settings → Clear Saved Quotes.
  • You can reset seen quote history in Settings → Reset Shuffle Memory.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately and permanently removes all stored data, preferences, and cached wallpapers from your device.
